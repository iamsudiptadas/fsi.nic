About Forest Survey of India

India's premier forest monitoring and research institution

Established

1 June 1981, Dehradun — succeeding the Preinvestment Survey of Forest Resources (PISFR) project of 1965.

Mandate

Biennial assessment of forest and tree cover, national forest inventory, and training in forest survey and remote sensing.

Headquarters

Kaulagarh Road, P.O. IPE, Dehradun – 248 195, Uttarakhand. Under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.

Regional Offices

Four regional offices at Shimla, Kolkata, Nagpur and Bengaluru support field inventory and data collection.

Key Functions

Forest cover assessment: Biennial wall-to-wall mapping from satellite imagery, published as the India State of Forest Report (ISFR).

National Forest Inventory: Field-based estimates of growing stock, biomass and carbon across forest types and trees outside forests.

Training and research: In-service training for forest officers and applied research in remote sensing, GIS and forest health.

Leadership

FSI is headed by a Director General, supported by Additional and Deputy Directors General across the technical, IT and administration divisions.
